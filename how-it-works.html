A thorough path from request to curb.

Clear sections for trip logistics, post-booking transitions, and stricter data habits than a typical booking form.

1

Quote & logistics submission

The rider, family coordinator, or facility scheduler submits pickup and drop-off cities, travel date, and mobility class. Automated validation blocks diagnoses, medications, and member IDs, generating a confirmation code (Example).

2

Dispatch & vehicle matching

Operations reviews Illinois route coverage and assigns the exact vehicle required: a hydraulic lift van for wheelchairs, a locking stretcher deck, or a low step-in sedan.

3

Door-through-door transport

Drivers with 20 years of experience verify the rider's name on the trip sheet, assist directly from the interior doorway, secure mobility equipment, and escort the rider into the facility reception.

4

Post-trip & secondary workflows

Once drop-off is logged, coordinators move seamlessly to return-trip scheduling, standing dialysis series, or facility account billing, while clinical charts remain isolated inside the healthcare provider's system.

Operational standards

Who can book
The rider, a family coordinator, or a facility scheduler. We do not need a chart.
Lead time
Same-day when a vehicle is free; standing routes are better booked 48 hours ahead.
What we need
Name for the driver to ask for, phone, cities, time window, mobility class.
What we refuse
Diagnoses, medications, insurance member IDs, Social Security numbers, photos of the rider.
At the curb
Driver confirms the name on the trip sheet. No clinical assessment.
After the trip
A completion time is logged. Clinical notes are never written by H.T.L Mobility.
Who drives
Drivers have 20 years of experience with wheelchair, stretcher, and ambulatory trips.
Where
Service covers all of Illinois.
