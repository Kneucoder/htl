Privacy

What this website keeps.

Short on purpose. Search engines may index the public pages. The ride form does not save what you type.

Public pages: browser, approximate region from your connection, and the page you opened. Used to keep the site up and to understand which services people read.

Scheduling form: validated in the browser, then discarded. Do not type diagnoses, account numbers, or other health details. A production desk would publish a separate notice before any electronic storage.

We do not sell contact lists. We do not place rider photos on the site. Emergency calls do not belong here — use 911.
