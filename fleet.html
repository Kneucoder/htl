Every vehicle is matched to the reservation. Choose a mobility level to see what arrives at the curb.

Lift van

For riders who stay seated. The lift raises the chair straight into the van.

  • Side or rear lift
  • Wheelchair securement
  • High roof
  • Marked in cardinal red, no emergency lighting

Stretcher unit

For riders who cannot sit. Used only when the reservation says stretcher.

  • Locking deck
  • Attendant jump seat
  • Climate control
  • Oxygen only when the facility has already arranged it

Ambulatory sedan

For riders who walk with a cane, walker, or a steady arm.

  • Low step-in
  • Trunk space for a folded walker
  • Same privacy rules as the vans
  • No clinical procedures en route
