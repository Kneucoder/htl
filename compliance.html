Compliance

HIPAA as a layout decision.

H.T.L Mobility is a transportation company, not a covered clinic. When a facility shares trip details with us, we still design as if the minimum-necessary rule applies.

Minimum necessary on public forms

The inquiry asks for a name the driver can call, a phone, two cities, a time, and a mobility class. There is no diagnosis, medication, or payer-ID field.

No PHI in the address bar

Pages are static marketing routes. Trip details are not placed in URLs, which would leak into server logs and browser history.

Nothing stored from this form

Submitting the form returns a reference code only. Details are not saved to a database. A live dispatch desk would use a vendor under a business associate agreement.

Role separation

Marketing copy, trip logistics, and any future clinical attachments stay in different systems. This website is the marketing system.

Access and notice

The privacy notice states what a production desk would collect, how long trip sheets are kept, and who to call to amend a record.

Workforce habit

Drivers carry a trip sheet, not a chart. Radios and texts stay on first name and address. Photos of riders are prohibited.

This page is an operational design statement, not legal advice and not a Notice of Privacy Practices for a specific covered entity.
